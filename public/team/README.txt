Team & advisor photos
=====================

Drop portrait images here and they appear automatically on /team
(until a file exists, a themed monogram is shown instead).

Use these exact filenames (square images work best, e.g. 600x600):

Founding team
  founder.jpg
  cofounder.jpg

Advisors
  arvind.jpg
  ganesh.jpg
  ramakrishnan.jpg
  vivek.jpg

To change a filename, edit the `src` value in app/team/page.jsx.
